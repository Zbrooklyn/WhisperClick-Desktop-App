# App Directories & Listing Sites for WhisperClick

> Last updated: 2026-03-17
>
> WhisperClick is a free, open-source, cross-platform voice-to-text desktop app.
> Public repo: https://github.com/Zbrooklyn/WhisperClick-Desktop-App

This document catalogs every directory, listing site, awesome list, and community
where WhisperClick should be submitted for discoverability. Items are grouped by
category with submission details and priority ratings.

Priority key:
- **HIGH** -- large audience, strong SEO value, or perfect category fit
- **MEDIUM** -- solid value but smaller audience or more effort
- **LOW** -- niche audience or diminishing returns, do after high/medium

## 2. GitHub Awesome Lists

Awesome lists are curated, community-maintained lists on GitHub. Submission is via
pull request. Each list has its own contribution guidelines (usually a
`CONTRIBUTING.md` or instructions in the README).

**General PR process:**
1. Fork the repo
2. Add WhisperClick in the appropriate section (maintain alphabetical order)
3. Follow the list's formatting exactly (usually `- [Name](URL) - Description.`)
4. Submit a PR with a short explanation of why the project fits
5. Maintainer reviews and merges (or requests changes)

## 5. Submission Copy

Reusable copy for directory submissions. Adjust length to fit each site's limits.

### Short Description (one-liner, ~80 chars)
> Voice-to-text desktop app -- press a hotkey, talk, text appears at your cursor.

### Medium Description (~150 chars)
> WhisperClick turns your voice into text anywhere on your desktop. Press a global hotkey, speak naturally, and the transcription auto-pastes at your cursor. Free and open source.

### Full Description (~250 chars)
> WhisperClick is a desktop app that turns your voice into text anywhere on your screen. Press a hotkey, talk, and the transcription pastes at your cursor. Works with OpenAI, Gemini, or fully offline. Open source, cross-platform, privacy-first.

### Feature Bullets (for directories that allow lists)
- Global hotkey works from any app -- no window switching
- Auto-paste at cursor position -- transcription goes right where you are typing
- Multiple AI backends: OpenAI (GPT-4o Transcribe, Whisper), Google Gemini, or local offline models
- Privacy-first: no telemetry, no analytics; local mode keeps audio on your machine
- Floating pill widget shows recording state without disrupting your workflow
- Searchable transcription history with original audio playback
- Cross-platform: Windows, macOS, Linux
- Open source (MIT license)
