# AlternativeTo Listing

## App Name

WhisperClick

## Short Description

Open-source desktop voice-to-text app. Press a hotkey from any application, speak, and the transcribed text pastes at your cursor. Supports OpenAI, Google Gemini, and local Whisper models for fully offline use.

## Full Description

WhisperClick turns your voice into text anywhere on your desktop. It runs in your system tray and gives you a global hotkey that works in every application -- email, Slack, VS Code, a terminal, Google Docs, anything with a text cursor.

Press the hotkey, talk naturally, press it again. The transcription appears right where you were typing. No window switching, no copy-paste, no separate dictation app to deal with.

**Transcription options:**
- OpenAI (GPT-4o Transcribe, GPT-4o Mini Transcribe, Whisper-1)
- Google Gemini (2.5 Flash, 2.5 Pro, and newer models)
- Local models via faster-whisper (fully offline, no internet required)

**Key features:**
- Global hotkey from any app (customizable)
- Auto-paste at cursor position
- 50+ languages with auto-detection
- Real-time translate (speak in one language, get text in another)
- Searchable history with audio playback
- Floating pill recording indicator
- Dark and light themes
- Auto-updates
- Cross-platform: Windows, macOS, Linux

**Privacy:** No telemetry, no analytics, no data collection. In local mode, your voice never leaves your computer. API keys are encrypted at rest.

Free for personal use. Open source under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## Alternative To

- **Dragon NaturallySpeaking** -- WhisperClick is free, open source, cross-platform, and uses modern AI models (GPT-4o, Whisper). Dragon costs $200+ and is Windows-only.
- **Windows Voice Typing (Win+H)** -- WhisperClick works in all apps (not just some), supports 50+ languages, offers model choice, keeps transcription history, and runs on macOS/Linux too.
- **Google Voice Typing (Docs)** -- WhisperClick works as a desktop app in any application, not just the browser. Supports offline mode and doesn't require a Google account.
- **Apple Dictation** -- WhisperClick gives you choice of transcription model, searchable history, and works on Windows/Linux as well.
- **Otter.ai** -- WhisperClick is a desktop app focused on real-time dictation (not meeting transcription). Free, offline-capable, no subscription.
- **Whisper.cpp** -- WhisperClick wraps Whisper in a polished desktop UI with auto-paste, history, and cloud provider options. No command line needed.
- **Talon Voice** -- WhisperClick focuses specifically on voice-to-text dictation rather than full voice computer control. Simpler to set up for people who just want to talk instead of type.
- **Nerd Dictation** -- WhisperClick is cross-platform with a GUI, auto-paste, and supports cloud transcription in addition to local models.
